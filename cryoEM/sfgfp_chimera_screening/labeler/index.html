Micrographs

Dashed outline = held-out test micrograph (never used for training). Filled = marked done.

How to label

Click the centre of the object to place the current label; click an existing mark to remove it. Label every DNA-bearing object of types 1–5 on the micrograph, then press D. Isolated sfGFP dots need no label: they are counted automatically. Type 6 is optional.

Scale: 1 display px = 2.84 Å; the yellow bar is 100 Å (35 px). A closed loop is ~86–100 Å across (~30–35 px) with one dark dot on its rim; free 50 bp DNA is a ~170 Å thread (~60 px).
